## 1. COMPANY VISION

### Why Friendli Tripz Exists
Travel is supposed to create connection, wonder, and lasting memories. Yet modern group travel has become transactional, anxiety-inducing, and fragmented. Aggregator platforms (OTAs) list thousands of unvetted options with zero human curation or ground coordination. Local operators rely on chaotic WhatsApp chats, hidden fees, and manual paper vouchers.

**Friendli Tripz** exists to restore joy, safety, and effortless social connection to group travel. We bridge the gap between digital discovery and real-world execution by combining hand-crafted social trip curation with an enterprise operational infrastructure.

### Mission
To deliver unforgettable, stress-free, and transparent group travel experiences by pairing hand-curated itineraries and verified cohorts with real-time ground logistics automation.

### Vision
To become South Asia’s most trusted social travel platform and the operational backbone for modern experiential tourism.

### Core Values
1. **Uncompromising Authenticity**: No fake reviews, no hidden charges, no commercial mass-market compromises. Every stay, route, and activity is physically verified.
2. **Radical Transparency**: Clear per-person pricing, explicit inclusion/exclusion manifests, and real-time operational status visibility.
3. **Safety & Cohort Trust**: Verified traveller profiles, cryptographic trip passes, live GPS vehicle tracking, and 24/7 incident response.
4. **Operational Excellence**: Frictionless dispatch for drivers, seamless check-in for hotels, and single-click administration for agency teams.
5. **Empathy for the Traveller**: Technology exists to serve human joy, not to create digital barriers.

### Brand Promise
"Kodaikanal (and every escape) hits different with the right people." When you book with Friendli Tripz, your itinerary is guaranteed, your stays are vetted, your logistics are automated, and your memories belong to you.

### What Success Looks Like
- **Customer Satisfaction**: >95% Net Promoter Score (NPS) and 40%+ repeat/referral booking rate.
- **Operational Precision**: 0% missed driver pickups, 0% hotel room allocation errors, and <5 minute enquiry response time.
- **Business Performance**: Sustainable unit economics, zero revenue leakage via automated ledger accounting, and rapid destination expansion.

### Long-Term Dream
To build a global network of social travel chapters where anyone can step into any destination worldwide and instantly experience curated, safe, and deeply social exploration.

## 2. OUR STORY

### Who We Are
Friendli Tripz was founded by passionate travellers, engineers, and hospitality operators who believed that group trips shouldn't require weeks of tedious planning, risky upfront bank transfers, or second-guessing hotel quality. We are a team of curators, technologists, and logistics directors headquartered in Tamil Nadu, India.

### Why We Started
The spark for Friendli Tripz came during a trip to Kodaikanal. A group of friends faced misleading hotel photos, uncommunicative driver services, and unexpected hidden charges for basic sightseeing routes. We realized that millions of young professionals and friend groups experience the exact same frustration every weekend. 

We set out to build a platform that handles every moving piece—from AI itinerary building to live driver dispatch and offline mobile passes—so travellers can simply show up and bond.

### Why Travel Matters
Travel breaks routine, broadens perspectives, and builds lifelong friendships. In an increasingly digital and isolated world, shared real-world experiences in nature (misty hills, tea plantations, forest trails) are vital for human well-being.

### Why Friendli Tripz Is Different
- **OTAs** sell static room nights and unvetted driver contacts.
- **Traditional Tour Agencies** deliver rigid, outdated banner tours for large bus crowds.
- **Friendli Tripz** delivers hand-crafted social micro-group escapes, transparent customization, live trip tracking, and cryptographically verified offline passes.

### Why Customers Trust Us
1. **Direct Ownership of Logistics**: We manage our vehicle rosters, hotel partnerships, and tour leaders directly.
2. **Immutable Ledger Accounting**: Guaranteed financial safety and instant booking receipts.
3. **Real-Time Visibility**: Track your driver, view live check-in status, and access emergency support at any second.

## 3. BRAND IDENTITY

### Brand Personality
Warm, Adventurous, Trustworthy, Modern, Social, and Meticulous.

### Voice & Tone
- **Voice**: Friendly, clear, confident, and inspiring. We speak like an experienced local friend who knows all the best secret spots.
- **Tone**: Adapts to context:
  - *Public Web / Social*: Energetic, inviting, evocative.
  - *Booking / Checkout*: Reassuring, precise, clear.
  - *Operations / Admin*: Crisp, objective, actionable.

### Writing Style
- Active voice over passive voice.
- Short, punchy headlines.
- Zero fluff, jargon, or generic travel cliches ("paradise on earth", "nestled in nature").
- Specific, grounded detail ("10.2381° N · Western Ghats", "3 Days / 2 Nights", "Handpicked Heritage Stay").

### Emotional Feeling
Refreshed, Confident, Eager, Safe, and Valued.

### Photography & Illustration Style
- **Photography**: High-resolution, authentic, warm-toned natural lighting. Features real travellers interacting, scenic landscapes, and authentic stay details. No generic stock photos.
- **Illustration**: Clean geometric lines, subtle topo/route map overlays, and crisp icons.

### Color Philosophy
Derived from the Western Ghats landscape:
- **Brand Navy (`#0B192C`)**: Dominance, stability, institutional trust, night sky over hilltops.
- **Brand Orange (`#FF6B35`)**: Energy, warmth, campfires, sunrise over mist, call to action.
- **Brand Warm Cream (`#FDFBF7`)**: Approachable background, organic canvas, natural paper feel.
- **Brand Soft Neutral (`#F3EFE0`)**: Subtle contrast, card borders, tag backgrounds.

### Typography Philosophy
- **Headings**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (Font weight 700/800/900). Strong, bold tracking for headlines.
- **Body Text**: `Inter` (Font weight 400/500/600). Crisp readability across screens.
- **Monospace Accent**: System Mono (`ui-monospace`). Used for GPS coordinates, timestamps, and cryptographic codes.

### Logo Usage
- Primary Lockup: Friendli emblem icon + "Friendli Tripz" typography + "Travel. Vibe. Repeat." sub-tagline.
- Minimum size: 32px height on digital screens.
- Always maintain clear space surrounding the logo equal to 50% of logo height.

### Brand Dos & Don'ts
- **DO**: Use real destination coordinates, transparent pricing, and specific activity names.
- **DO**: Emphasize group safety, cohort verification, and local expertise.
- **DON'T**: Use low-quality images, hidden fees, vague itineraries, or misleading discount banners.
- **DON'T**: Use corporate developer jargon in customer-facing views ("Execute transaction", "Entity record modified").

## 5. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

### Why Every Feature Exists
Every line of code, database table, and UI component must serve one of five primary goals:
1. **Conversion**: Help travellers understand value and book confidently.
2. **Trust**: Demonstrate transparency, safety, and authenticity.
3. **Operational Efficiency**: Eliminate manual effort for agency teams.
4. **Reliability**: Ensure zero downtime, fail-fast resilience, and offline access.
5. **Customer Satisfaction**: Deliver a flawless trip experience from departure to return.

### How Friendli Tripz Differs from OTAs
OTAs act as passive bulletin boards. Friendli Tripz acts as an active, integrated travel execution system. We own the quality outcome of the trip.

### Why Operations Matter
A beautiful booking site means nothing if the driver is 2 hours late or the hotel has no record of the reservation. Operations is the real product. That is why we built dedicated portals for Drivers (`/driver`), Hotels (`/hotel-portal`), Activity Vendors (`/vendor-portal`), and Tour Captains (`/tour-leader`).

### Why Transparency & Trust Matter
Unexpected charges kill customer trust forever. We display exact breakdown pricing, inclusion lists, exclusion lists, and cancellation terms up front.

### Why WhatsApp Matters
In South Asia, WhatsApp is the primary communication channel. We integrate direct WhatsApp actions and automated alerts throughout the enquiry, booking, and trip lifecycle.

### Why Customization Matters
No two groups are identical. The Trip Customizer (`/customize`) allows travellers to tailor dates, stays, and activities without losing the convenience of a managed package.

## 8. WEBSITE CONTENT STRATEGY

### Core Content Directives
1. **Show, Don't Just Tell**: Replace generic adjectives with concrete details (e.g. write "Handpicked heritage bungalow with valley view balconies" instead of "Luxurious accommodation").
2. **Local Ground Expertise**: Incorporate geographical markers (altitude, route distance, weather windows) to establish authority.
3. **Transparent Pricing**: Always display per-person pricing alongside clear group size modifiers.
4. **Humanizing Social Travel**: Highlight small cohort sizes (8-14 travellers), age brackets, and social activities (bonfires, group treks, stargazing).

## 10. END-TO-END CUSTOMER JOURNEY

```
1. DISCOVERY ➔ 2. BROWSING ➔ 3. CUSTOMIZATION ➔ 4. ENQUIRY ➔ 5. SALES CONTACT
                                                                     │
8. TRIP EXECUTION ◄── 7. PREPARATION ◄── 6. BOOKING & PAYMENT ◄──────┘
       │
       ▼
9. REVIEW & REWARDS
```

1. **Discovery**: User arrives via organic search, social recommendation, or referral link.
2. **Browsing**: User evaluates destination guides (`/destinations/kodaikanal`) and package details (`/packages/misty-kodaikanal-escape`).
3. **Customization**: User modifies dates, group size, and meal options in `/customize`.
4. **Enquiry**: User submits customized preferences; an automated lead entry is logged in `enquiries`.
5. **Sales Contact**: Sales agent contacts user via WhatsApp/Call, refines itinerary details, and generates direct payment link.
6. **Booking & Payment**: User completes payment via Razorpay. An immutable `booking_snapshot` and ledger entry are written.
7. **Preparation**: User accesses `/trip/[id]` to view driver details, hotel confirmation codes, and save the offline PWA pass.
8. **Trip Execution**: User presents HMAC QR code at hotel (`/hotel-portal`) and activity points (`/vendor-portal`); tracks vehicle position in real-time.
9. **Review & Rewards**: User receives post-trip survey, submits review on `/reviews`, and receives referral points on `/loyalty`.

## 12. PARTNER JOURNEY

### 1. Driver Journey (`/driver`)
- **Duty Notification**: Driver receives SMS/WhatsApp ping with PIN login link.
- **Morning Duty Start**: Driver opens `/driver`, enters PIN, clicks "Start Duty", and GPS pings initialize.
- **Pickup Execution**: Driver views guest pickup locations, clicks "Guest Boarded", and navigates via Google Maps link.
- **Duty Complete**: Driver clicks "Complete Trip"; duty log writes to `driver_duty_logs`.

### 2. Hotel Partner Journey (`/hotel-portal`)
- **Manifest View**: Hotel front desk opens `/hotel-portal` to view today's incoming guest arrivals.
- **Check-in Verification**: Front desk verifies guest booking reference or scans offline pass.
- **Check-in Complete**: Front desk clicks "Confirm Check-in", updating room status in real-time.

### 3. Vendor Partner Journey (`/vendor-portal`)
- **Voucher Scan**: Activity operator opens `/vendor-portal` scanner on mobile.
- **QR Validation**: Operator scans traveller's HMAC QR voucher.
- **Validation Result**: Instant GREEN tick (Valid) or RED alert (Already Used / Invalid); settlement entry logged.

### 4. Tour Captain Journey (`/tour-leader`)
- **Group Broadcast**: Captain opens `/tour-leader` to send group schedule updates (e.g. "Dinner at 8:00 PM").
- **Incident Logging**: In case of medical or weather delay, Captain logs incident, alerting Admin instantly.

## 15. CONTENT WRITING GUIDE

### Tone Rules
1. **Be Specific**: Always state exact numbers, days, and inclusions.
2. **Be Reassuring**: Emphasize safety, direct support, and zero hidden costs.
3. **Be Inviting**: Use welcoming, active language.
