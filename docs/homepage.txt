I think this is a **very strong direction**, but I would make one important strategic change.

Your slogan is:

> **Travel. Vibe. Repeat.**
> *Stop scrolling. Start living.*

This doesn't position Friendli Tripz as a **travel booking website**—it positions it as a **travel lifestyle brand**, which is exactly where brands like Airbnb, Hostelworld, and GetYourGuide succeed. However, the homepage should balance **emotion** with **conversion**.

---

# I'd evolve it into this narrative

## 1. Hero — Inspire

Don't sell destinations.
Don't sell packages.

Sell the transformation.

> **Travel. Vibe. Repeat.**
>
> **Stop scrolling. Start living.**
>
> Weekend escapes, curated group trips, and unforgettable experiences with people you'll actually enjoy traveling with.

Large CTA:

🟧 Explore Trips

Secondary:

⚪ Plan My Trip

---

## 2. Choose Your Vibe — Help visitors identify themselves

This is your differentiator.

Instead of asking "Where do you want to go?"

Ask:

> **How do you want to feel?**

Examples:

* 🌄 Mountain Escape
* ☕ Slow Weekend
* 🚗 Road Trip
* 🏕 Campfire Nights
* 🌊 Beach Break
* 🎉 Friends Getaway
* ❤️ Couple Escape
* 🌿 Nature Reset

This makes Friendli memorable.

---

Not stock photography.

Instead of another generic booking prompt:

*Travel. Vibe. Repeat.*

🟧 Join the Next Trip

⚪ Explore Destinations

# Visual direction

Your current screenshots show a clean interface, but they still resemble a polished travel agency. To reinforce the lifestyle positioning:

* Use a cinematic full-width hero video or an animated collage of multiple destinations rather than one static destination.
* Introduce subtle motion: floating destination cards, moving map pins, traveler avatars, and counters that animate into view.
* Increase whitespace and simplify section backgrounds to let imagery stand out.
* Replace many rectangular cards with a mix of large and small cards for a more editorial feel.
* Feature authentic traveler photography instead of generic scenic landscapes wherever possible.

---

# One thing I would change

I would rename **"Choose Your Vibe"** to something slightly more premium.

Some alternatives:

* **Find Your Vibe** ⭐ (my favorite)
* **Choose Your Escape**
* **Travel Your Way**
* **How Do You Want to Feel?**
* **Start With a Vibe**
* **What's Your Weekend Mood?**

"Find Your Vibe" is short, memorable, and naturally ties into **Travel. Vibe. Repeat.**

## Final homepage flow

1. **Hero** – *Travel. Vibe. Repeat.*
2. **Find Your Vibe** – emotional travel categories
3. **Trending Escapes** – destination cards
4. **Upcoming Trips** – departure calendar
5. **Why Travel Friendli?** – experience-focused benefits
6. **Moments That Matter** – real traveler gallery
7. **Stories Worth Sharing** – modern testimonials
8. **How It Works** – four simple steps
9. **Travel With Confidence** – trust and support
10. **FAQs**
11. **Final CTA** – *Your Next Story Starts Here.*
12. **Footer**

This structure creates a clear emotional journey: **emotion → identity → destination → trust → action**. Instead of feeling like a catalog of packages, Friendli Tripz becomes a brand people want to be part of.
